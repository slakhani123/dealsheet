How to use this

Four steps, every Monday morning. Takes about ten minutes between the three of you.
1

Say who you are

Top right of the page. Your name goes on everything you change, so the team can see who moved what.

2

Work the four piles

The tiles below filter the book: gone quiet, no figure yet, action due, not yet reviewed. Click one to see only those.

3

Update your deals

Click any deal to open it. Set the owner, stage, status and — the one that matters — the next action and when it is due.

4

Mark it reviewed

Hit Mark reviewed and the bar at the top fills. Nothing to do is a real answer — mark it reviewed and move on.

The figures are read-only. Loan amounts, LTVs and dates come from the deal sheet and refresh from Shyam's inbox — if one is wrong, fix the deal sheet, and leave a note here so nobody acts on it meanwhile. Everything else is yours: owner, stage, status, next action and notes are only ever changed by the three of you. Took an enquiry on the phone? It will not be in the sheet — put it straight on the board with + Add deal.
